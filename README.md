# Fortune in a Pot — a fortune for a little fern

Entry for the make.fun bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9

> Three new leaves by spring, little fern: the tall window is yours, and the cat has already been informed.

## Why this line

A fortune has to promise something specific, and this one promises the three things a windowsill fern actually wants: growth ("three new leaves by spring" — a number, a deadline, a season to look forward to), territory ("the tall window is yours" — the best light on the sill, awarded outright), and safety, delivered as the joke: "the cat has already been informed" treats the household cat as a rival tenant who has been formally served notice. It reads kind first and funny second, it is unmistakably about the plant, and at 105 characters it sits comfortably inside the bounty's 120-character cap.

`main.py` holds the line, asserts it against the cap, writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and reads the file back to prove the bytes match.
